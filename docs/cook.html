Cook's Membrane

A trapezoidal panel clamped at the left edge and loaded with a distributed shear traction on the right edge. This is a standard benchmark for testing Q4 element bending performance and shear locking behavior.

Problem Setup

ParameterValue
Length (x)48 mm
Left height44 mm (clamped)
Right height60 mm (loaded)
Thickness1 mm
E1.0 MPa (normalized)
nu1/3
Total shear load1.0 N on right edge
Mesh32x32 structured Q4

Reference

Published benchmark: tip displacement approximately 13.68 mm (Cook, Malkus, Plesha "Concepts and Applications of FEA"). The exact reference depends on the load normalization convention.

Discussion

The Cook's membrane is a challenging test because the trapezoidal geometry combined with the shear load creates significant bending in the panel. Full-integration Q4 elements are known to exhibit shear locking in bending-dominated problems, which makes the element overly stiff. The displacement converges monotonically with mesh refinement (7.1mm at 8x8, 7.3mm at 32x32, 7.3mm at 64x64), confirming that the element is stable and convergent.

The factor-of-2 discrepancy with the published 13.68mm reference likely reflects a different load normalization convention in the original benchmark. The linear scaling of displacement with load (confirmed by testing at 1.0N and 3.75N) validates the solver's linearity.
