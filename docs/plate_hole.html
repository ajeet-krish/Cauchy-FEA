Plate with Circular Hole

A classic stress concentration problem validated against the Kirsch analytical solution. A large plate with a central circular hole under uniaxial tension develops a stress concentration factor (SCF) of 3.0 at the hole edge.

Problem Setup

ParameterValue
Domain[0, 2] x [0, 2] (quarter-symmetry)
HoleRadius R=0.5 at origin (0, 0)
MaterialSteel: E=200 GPa, nu=0.3, t=0.01m
Left edge (x=0)ux=0 (symmetry)
Bottom edge (y=0)uy=0 (symmetry)
Right edge (x=2)sigma_inf=1 MPa tension
Mesh16x16 structured Q4 with hole cutout

Kirsch Solution

For an infinite plate with a circular hole under uniaxial tension:
sigma_max = 3 * sigma_inf at theta = 90 deg (hole edge)
This is the theoretical stress concentration factor (SCF = 3.0).

Discussion

The quarter-symmetry model reduces the problem size by a factor of 4. Nodes inside the circular hole are removed from the mesh, and symmetry boundary conditions are applied on the cut edges. The SCF at the hole edge should approach 3.0 as the mesh is refined, though the staircase approximation of the circular boundary on a structured quad mesh introduces geometric error that limits accuracy on coarse meshes.
